Study Notes

Physics

Motion: Speed = distance/time. Velocity is speed in a specified direction. Acceleration is change in velocity per unit time.

Force: Newton's second law is F = ma. Weight near Earth is W = mg.

Work and energy: Work = force × displacement in the direction of force. Kinetic energy = ½mv². Power = work/time.

Chemistry

Atomic structure: Atomic number is proton number; mass number is protons + neutrons. Isotopes have the same proton number but different neutron numbers.

Bonding: Ionic bonding involves attraction between oppositely charged ions; covalent bonding involves shared electron pairs.

Acids and bases: At 25°C, neutral aqueous solution has pH 7. Acids increase hydronium ion concentration in water.

Biology

Cell: The cell is the basic unit of life. Ribosomes make proteins; mitochondria are a major site of aerobic respiration.

Genetics: DNA stores hereditary information. Mitosis generally produces two genetically similar daughter cells; meiosis produces haploid cells.

Human physiology: Alveoli are sites of gas exchange. Nephrons are functional units of kidneys.

English Academy

Beginner: Parts of speech

Noun: names a person, place, thing, or idea. Example: student.

Verb: shows an action or state. Example: learn.

Adjective: describes a noun. Example: useful.

Grammar: Present simple

Use the present simple for habits and facts: “I study every day.” With he/she/it, commonly add -s: “She studies.”

Vocabulary

Improve = make better • Explain = make clear • Accurate = correct • Practice = repeated learning.

Practice

Choose the correct sentence: “He studies biology.” The verb agrees with the third-person singular subject.
